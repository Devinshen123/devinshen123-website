Summary of the Data Analyst, Advanced Analytics job at Liberty Mutual

Liberty Mutual is hiring a Data Analyst, Advanced Analytics with 0 - 3 years of experience. Based in United States - Remote and with Remote ways of working. The expected salary range for this role is $67,000 - $126,000
About Liberty Mutual

At Liberty Mutual, we believe progress happens when people feel secure. For more than 110 years we have helped people and businesses embrace today and confidently pursue tomorrow by providing protection for the unexpected and delivering it with care.

A Fortune 100 company with more than 40,000 employees in 28 countries and economies, we are the ninth largest global property and casualty insurer and generate more than $50 billion in annual consolidated revenue.

We operate through three strategic business units: US Retail Markets, providing auto, home, renters and other personal and small commercial lines property and casualty insurance to individuals and small businesses countrywide; Global Risk Solutions, delivering a full range of comprehensive commercial and specialty insurance, reinsurance and surety solutions to mid-size and large businesses worldwide; and Liberty Mutual Investments, deploying more than $100 billion of long-term capital globally across its integrated platform to drive economic growth, power innovation and secure Liberty Mutual’s promises.

Data Analyst, Advanced Analytics job description

Description

The Strategy and Insights team within the Enterprise Transformation Office is actively searching for a highly motivated analyst to join the Procurement Analytics team. This position will provide analytical support and insights to Enterprise Procurement to help generate value from their supplier relationships. This person will be required to frame and answer operational and strategic questions from senior management and executive leadership. The ideal candidate possesses advanced analytical abilities and will be able to work collaboratively & independently.

Responsibilities:

Perform analysis and recommendations to influence Enterprise Procurement strategies and goals.

May be asked to present analysis in an understandable and concise manner to effectively influence others.

With guidance, develop and produce dashboards and reports to support assessment and monitoring of operational and expense/spend metrics.

Assist in maintaining and improving the current reporting infrastructure.

Identify issues within existing tools, processes, data sources and voice suggestions to the team to improve accuracy, decrease latency, and reduce inefficiencies.

Stay informed about procurement category market trends, identifying drivers for expense saving opportunities.

Maintains close working relationships with data providers and partners.

Query and validate large and complex datasets to deliver analytically sound insights.

Support an inclusive, equitable, and high-performing culture.

Data Analyst, Advanced Analytics job requirements

Qualifications:

Bachelor's Degree plus 2-4 years of experience, or master’s degree with no professional experience, or equivalent.

Proficient in Excel, PowerPoint, and statistical software packages (SAS, Emblem).

Knowledgeable in data query languages and tools (SQL and Snowflake)

Must have good planning, analytical, decision-making and communication skills.

Knowledge of data sources, tools, statistical principles, methodologies, and techniques.

What we offer at Liberty Mutual

At Liberty Mutual, our goal is to create a workplace where everyone feels valued, supported, and can thrive. We build an environment that welcomes a wide range of perspectives and experiences, with inclusion embedded in every aspect of our culture and reflected in everyday interactions. This comes to life through comprehensive benefits, workplace flexibility, professional development opportunities, and a host of opportunities provided through our Employee Resource Groups. Each employee plays a role in creating our inclusive culture, which supports every individual to do their best work.

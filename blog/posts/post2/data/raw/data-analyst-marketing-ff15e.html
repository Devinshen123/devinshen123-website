About NinjaOne

NinjaOne unifies IT to simplify work for nearly 40,000 customers in 140+ countries. ​

NinjaOne is #1 in 14 categories in the G2 Winter Report, including autonomous endpoint management, patch management, RMM, and Online Backup.

Data Analyst, Marketing job description

About the Role:

As the Marketing Data Analyst at NinjaOne, you will be a key member of our global marketing team. You will support our global marketing team in evaluating the performance and business health of paid and non-paid campaigns and initiatives. The team will look to your insights across our datasets to investigate complex questions that come up. You will work with the content and performance marketing teams to help manage and optimize the performance of our marketing initiatives.

You are a storyteller who knows how to use data to identify insights and help business stakeholders make timely and impactful decisions from your insights. You understand the importance of identifying both large-scale and incremental opportunities.

Location - We are flexible on remote working from home, if you are located in the USA and reside in one of the following states - CA, CO, OR, TX, and WA.

What You’ll be Doing:

Work with marketing teams to analyze daily, weekly, and monthly performance, and aid in the production of internal and external reporting and dashboards

Analyze enormous datasets from 10+ channels, 500+ campaigns, and a multimillion-dollar budget, and proactively discover opportunities for optimization and growth

Be responsible for data collection and data processing, and build and maintain a cadence of regular reports and analysis

Build self-serve data visualization and data reports for business stakeholders

Conduct full funnel, multi-channel ROI analysis, and attribution analysis

Other duties as assigned

Data Analyst, Marketing job requirements

About You

BA/BS degree in Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Machine Learning, Operations Research, Analytics, or Computer Science required (MS preferred)

1-3 years' experience in an analytics-focused role supporting marketing / website / business reporting and analysis functions.
SaaS company particularly in supporting marketing teams experience preferred

Experience pulling data from various sources and generating sophisticated excel reports

High attention to detail, stellar analytical skills, and the ability to make data-driven decisions

Experience with data visualization and analysis tools is a plus: Tableau, Databricks, Python (e.g. Jupyter notebooks, pandas)

You thrive in fast-paced environments, embracing ambiguity and complexity with ease

What we offer at NinjaOne

What You’ll Love :

We are a collaborative, kind, and curious community. 

We honor your flexibility needs with full-time work that is hybrid remote.

We have you covered with our comprehensive benefits package, which includes medical, dental, and vision insurance.

We help you prepare for your financial future with our 401(k) plan.

We prioritize your work-life balance with our unlimited PTO.

We reward your work with opportunity for growth and advancement.
